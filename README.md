# Lýdia Fúčela

I work on business transformation and practical AI adoption. My background is in corporate operations, process change and leading teams through large ERP and organizational projects. I now apply that experience to smaller businesses, where a useful AI workflow has to fit the work people actually do.

My starting point is simple: decide where a person should make the call, where a rule is enough, and where AI can help. Then design the process around that decision.

## What I work on

- Mapping work across sales, customer service and back-office teams.
- Assessing opportunities by business value, effort, ownership and adoption risk.
- Testing AI-assisted workflows with a person reviewing consequential decisions.
- Connecting business owners, delivery teams and the people who will use the result.

## Current case study

**AI-assisted lead research and outreach for a small business.** With Make, Tavily, OpenAI, Claude and Notion, I have been building a workflow that finds opportunities, scores them, checks for duplicates and prepares outreach for a person to review. The lead search is running as a pilot. I am now testing the outreach step, including a search for missing contact context. I have documented the decisions, tests and open issues in [the case study](https://github.com/lydiafucela/ai-lead-workflow-case-study).
